AIR QUALITY INDEX

Understanding PM2.5

PM2.5 indicates the concentration of fine particulate matter in the air.

0–35

Good

Air quality is considered healthy.

36–75

Moderate

Acceptable air quality for most people.

76–150

Unhealthy

Sensitive groups may experience effects.

150+

Very High

Health effects become more significant.

ABOUT AEROPURE

Machine Learning for Cleaner Air

AirNova analyzes the Beijing Multi-Site Air-Quality Dataset, containing hourly measurements from 12 monitoring stations between 2013 and 2017.

The system combines historical pollution measurements, weather conditions and temporal patterns to forecast future PM2.5 concentrations.
